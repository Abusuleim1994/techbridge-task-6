What is TechBridge?

TechBridge is a learning and experience program from Baselink Services Limited. It connects what you study with the kind of work done in real projects, so you finish with practical experience and not only theory.

Who is it for?

Learners and aspiring professionals who want hands-on experience in Data Analytics or Web Development.

Current programs

Data Analytics

Work with data to find patterns and answer questions through practical, project-based tasks.

Web Development

Learn to design and build real web pages through practical, project-based tasks.

The 30-Day Practical Internship

A focused month where you learn by doing. Both programs run as a practical internship.

  • 30 days of structured, practical work
  • Project-based tasks that reflect real work
  • Recommendation letter: a letter may be provided based on your successful participation and performance
